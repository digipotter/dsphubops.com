Roles we hire for

We hire for these roles as we grow. If nothing fits exactly, send your details anyway.

Operations coordinator

Keeps a client's week on track: tracks tasks across services, prepares the weekly report and is the day-to-day contact.

Helpful background: Organized, clear in writing, comfortable with spreadsheets.

Recruiting coordinator

Posts jobs, screens applicants, schedules interviews and moves candidates through checks and onboarding.

Helpful background: High-volume recruiting or scheduling experience.

Payroll and bookkeeping specialist

Reviews timecards, prepares payroll, reconciles accounts and client invoices, and helps close the month.

Helpful background: Payroll, bookkeeping or invoice experience; ADP, Paycom or QuickBooks a plus.

Dispatch support specialist

Monitors routes, tracks inspections and hours of service and writes the end-of-day summary.

Helpful background: Dispatch, logistics or customer operations experience.

HR and compliance coordinator

Keeps employee files complete, tracks I-9s, policy sign-offs and expiring licenses, and handles separation paperwork and unemployment claim responses.

Helpful background: HR administration or recordkeeping experience.

Scorecard and performance analyst

Tracks weekly scorecards, checks delivery defects and camera events against the evidence, prepares disputes for approval and drafts coaching notes.

Helpful background: Delivery operations, quality or claims experience.

Fleet compliance coordinator

Schedules maintenance, follows up on repairs and keeps vehicle records and driver qualification files audit-ready.

Helpful background: Fleet, maintenance or compliance experience.
