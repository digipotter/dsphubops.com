Driver performance and safety

Protect your scorecard and your drivers by spotting problems early, coaching fairly and keeping a clear record of what was done.

Get pricing Call 1-866-744-6870

Why safety slips

  • Scorecard dips that are only noticed after they cost you.
  • Camera events piling up without review.
  • Coaching that happens late, or only as discipline.
  • No record of who was coached and whether it helped.

What we handle

  • Weekly scorecard tracking and trends
  • Camera event review, for example Netradyne
  • Early flags for drivers who need coaching
  • Coaching notes prepared for your managers
  • Follow-up tracking after coaching
  • A list of drivers who improved, so good work gets noticed

Stays with you: coaching conversations and any disciplinary decisions.

How it runs

  1. 1

    Track the numbers

    Scorecard and safety metrics reviewed every week, with trends by driver.

  2. 2

    Review the events

    Camera events reviewed in context and sorted by what needs coaching.

  3. 3

    Support the coaching

    Notes and talking points for your managers, then a follow-up to see what changed.

In your weekly report

  • Scorecard trends
  • Drivers flagged for coaching
  • Coaching completed and follow-ups due
  • Drivers who improved

Questions

Do you talk to our drivers directly?

Usually no. We prepare the review and notes, and your managers have the conversations. We can adjust this to how your team works.

Which camera systems do you work with?

We review events in the camera platform you already use, for example Netradyne.

How do you keep reviews fair?

Every event is reviewed in context, and coaching notes stick to what happened rather than assumptions.
