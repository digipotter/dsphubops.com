Hiring and onboarding

Delivery fleets hire all year, and harder before peak. We run the admin around hiring so open roles fill faster and new drivers show up ready for their first route.

Get pricing Call 1-866-744-6870

What slows hiring down

  • Hours spent posting jobs and sorting resumes that don't fit.
  • Candidates who go quiet between the interview and the background check.
  • Paperwork that stalls a start date: missing forms, unsigned policies, late I-9s.
  • Training sessions with no-shows that waste a trainer's day.

What we handle

  • Job posts on the boards you use, under your company name
  • Resume screening against the criteria you set
  • Interview scheduling and reminders
  • Background check and drug test coordination with your vendors
  • I-9 and E-Verify paperwork
  • Offer letters and onboarding documents you provide
  • Training calendar and no-show follow-up

Stays with you: every hiring decision. We coordinate; you choose who joins your team.

How it runs

  1. 1

    Set your criteria

    You tell us what a good driver looks like for your operation: license, experience, location and schedule.

  2. 2

    We fill the pipeline

    We post, screen, schedule interviews and keep candidates moving through checks and paperwork.

  3. 3

    You choose

    You interview and decide. We send offers, onboarding documents and training invites for the people you pick.

In your weekly report

  • Open roles and applicants at each stage
  • Interviews booked and completed
  • Background checks and drug tests pending
  • New drivers scheduled for training

Questions

Do you make hiring decisions for us?

No. We screen against the criteria you set and handle the coordination. Interviews, offers and final decisions stay with you.

Can you post jobs under our company name?

Yes. Posts go out under your company name, on the job boards you prefer, with wording you approve.

Do you run the background checks?

We coordinate with the background check and drug test vendors you already use and track each candidate until their results are in.
