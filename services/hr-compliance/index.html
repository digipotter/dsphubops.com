HR and compliance documentation

Every driver and employee comes with paperwork that has to be complete and stay current. We keep your HR records in order, track what is about to expire and have files ready when someone asks for them.

Get pricing Call 1-855-DSP-HUB1

Where HR paperwork breaks down

  • Employee files missing signed policies, licenses or I-9 documents.
  • Driver's licenses and certifications that expire without anyone noticing.
  • Attendance, write-ups and leave tracked in different places, or not at all.
  • Separations handled without the paperwork a later claim or audit will ask for.

What we handle

  • Employee files set up and kept complete for every hire
  • I-9 tracking, including reverification dates
  • Policy and handbook acknowledgments collected and filed
  • Driver's license and certification expiration tracking
  • Attendance, write-up and leave records kept in one place
  • Separation checklists and final paperwork
  • A monthly file check with a list of gaps

Stays with you: employment decisions, discipline and anything that needs legal advice.

How it runs

  1. 1

    Check your files

    We review every employee file against a checklist you approve and list what is missing.

  2. 2

    Close the gaps

    We collect missing documents and signatures and set reminders for anything that expires.

  3. 3

    Keep it current

    New hires, changes and separations are filed as they happen, with a monthly check on top.

In your weekly report

  • Files missing documents
  • Licenses and documents expiring soon
  • Policy acknowledgments outstanding
  • Separations and their paperwork status

Questions

Do you give legal or HR advice?

No. We keep records and processes in order. Questions about employment law go to your HR advisor or attorney.

Where are employee files kept?

In your HR or payroll system, or the document storage you already use. We work inside your systems.

Can you help us prepare for an audit?

Yes. We review files against the request, list what is missing and help you collect it before the deadline.
