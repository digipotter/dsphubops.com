Services

Back-office work for Amazon Delivery Service Partners. Choose the services you need. Every decision about your people, pay and disputes stays with you.

01

Hiring and onboarding

Hiring never stops at a DSP. We handle the busy work around it so open roles get filled faster.

What we handle

  • Job posts on the boards you use, under your company name
  • Resume screening against the criteria you set
  • Interview scheduling and reminders
  • Background check and drug test coordination with your vendors
  • I-9 and E-Verify paperwork
  • Offer letters and onboarding documents you provide
  • Training calendar and no-show follow-up

Stays with you: every hiring decision. We coordinate; you choose who joins your team.

02

Payroll and bookkeeping

Payroll that runs on time, with the numbers checked before they go out.

What we handle

  • Setting up new hires in ADP or Paycom
  • Timecard checks before each payroll run
  • Payroll processing, with overtime and bonus reports
  • Bank and credit card reconciliation
  • A monthly profit and loss statement

Stays with you: approval of every payroll run.

03

Invoice audit and recovery

Missed rescues, cancelled routes and unpaid reimbursements add up. We check every invoice so you are paid for the work you did.

What we handle

  • Your weekly Amazon invoice checked against route data
  • Rescues, cancellations and vehicle reimbursements reviewed
  • Fleet lease bills checked line by line
  • Disputes filed and followed up
  • A weekly summary of what was found and recovered

Stays with you: the final say on every dispute we file.

04

Dispatch and station support

Support for your dispatch team from the first load-out until the last van is back.

What we handle

  • Route monitoring through the day
  • Early warning on routes that are falling behind
  • Pre-trip and post-trip inspection (DVIC) tracking
  • Hours-of-service checks
  • An end-of-day summary

Stays with you: calls on rescues and reassignments.

05

Driver performance and safety

Protect your scorecard by spotting problems before they show up in it.

What we handle

  • Weekly scorecard tracking
  • Camera event review, for example Netradyne
  • Early flags for drivers who need coaching
  • Coaching notes and follow-up tracking

Stays with you: coaching conversations and any disciplinary decisions.

06

Fleet and compliance

Keep vans on the road and your records ready for any audit.

What we handle

  • Preventive maintenance scheduling
  • Vehicle records and documents
  • Follow-up on grounded vehicles
  • Compliance files kept ready for an audit

Stays with you: repair approvals and vendor choices.

07

Unemployment claims

Unemployment claims come with tight deadlines. We keep track of every one.

What we handle

  • Claim tracking with due dates
  • Evidence gathered from payroll and records
  • Responses prepared for your review
  • Support with appeals when needed

Stays with you: sign-off on every response sent to the state.

Tell us what takes up most of your week and we’ll suggest the services that help most.
