Payroll and bookkeeping

Pay drivers correctly and on time, with the numbers checked before they go out, and keep books that show how the business is really doing.

Get pricing Call 1-866-744-6870

Where payroll goes wrong

  • Missed punches and timecard edits that nobody reviews.
  • Overtime, bonuses and incentives worked out by hand.
  • New hires set up late or with the wrong pay details.
  • Books that are months behind, so margins are a guess.

What we handle

  • New hires set up in ADP or Paycom
  • Timecard review before each payroll run
  • Overtime, bonus and incentive calculations checked
  • Payroll processing and reporting
  • Pay rate changes, deductions and PTO balances kept current
  • Year-end W-2 support with your payroll provider
  • Bank and credit card reconciliation
  • Vendor bills and expense categories
  • A monthly profit and loss statement

Stays with you: approval of every payroll run and every payment.

How it runs

  1. 1

    Connect your systems

    We get access to your payroll provider, timekeeping and bank feeds with our own logins.

  2. 2

    Check before each run

    Timecards, overtime and pay changes are reviewed and flagged before payroll is submitted.

  3. 3

    Close the month

    Reconciliations and a profit and loss statement each month, with notes on anything unusual.

In your weekly report

  • Payroll status and exceptions
  • Timecard issues found and fixed
  • Upcoming pay changes
  • Month-end close progress

Questions

Which payroll providers do you work with?

We work in ADP and Paycom, and can work with other providers on request.

Do you replace our accountant?

No. We keep the books current and organized so your accountant can do tax and advisory work faster.

Who approves payroll?

You do. We prepare and check it, and you approve it before it is submitted.
